Korean medicine treatments including blood stasis-removing therapy and auriculotherapy for persistent headache after traumatic brain injury: A case report

Kwon CY, Lee B, Leem J, Chung SY, Kim JW

EXPLORE 15(6):419-424

DOIPubMed

INT31EXPLORE

논문 소개Summary

외상성 뇌손상 뒤 이어진 두통을 어혈을 푸는 한약과 이침으로 치료한 74세 여성의 증례입니다. 자전거를 타다 넘어져 오른쪽 이마를 바위에 부딪고 10분가량 의식을 잃었으며, 뇌 CT에서 혈종 외에 이상은 없었으나 사고 뒤로 혼미함과 건망, 오른쪽 이마의 심한 두통을 겪었습니다. 보건소 치매예방교실에서 이침을 받은 뒤 두통이 숫자통증척도 8 이상에서 2로 떨어졌고, 당귀수산 엑스제를 하루 9 g 함께 쓰면서 통증 부위도 줄었습니다. 71일째 두통은 1까지 내려갔고 간이정신상태검사는 25점에서 27점이 되었으며 115일 추적에서도 유지되었습니다. 단일 증례이고 대조군이 없어 자연경과를 배제할 수 없습니다.

A case report of a 74-year-old woman whose persistent headache after traumatic brain injury was treated with blood stasis-removing herbal medicine and auriculotherapy. She had fallen from her bicycle, striking her right forehead on a rock and losing consciousness for about ten minutes; brain computed tomography showed a haematoma but no other abnormality, yet from the accident onward she suffered confusion, amnesia and severe headache localised to the right forehead. After auriculotherapy at a dementia prevention class in a public health centre, her headache fell from 8 or more on the numeric rating scale to 2, and the painful area shrank further once Dangguixu-san extract 9 g per day was added. By day 71 the headache had declined to 1 and her Mini-Mental State Examination score had risen from 25 to 27, both maintained at follow-up on day 115. With a single case and no control group, natural recovery cannot be ruled out.
