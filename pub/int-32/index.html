Impact of gender on the career development of female traditional Korean medicine doctors: a qualitative study

Chun SE, Lee JH, Lee JE, Lee SMK, Leem J, Kim H

BMJ Open 9(8):e030390

DOI

INT32BMJ Open

논문 소개Summary

여성 한의사가 늘고 있지만 성별이 그들의 경력에 어떤 영향을 주는지는 연구된 적이 없었습니다. 2017년 기준 한의사 중 여성 비율은 21%로 의사 25.4%, 세계 평균 46%보다 낮습니다. 30대 여성 한의사 열 명을 반구조화 면담으로 만나 경력 선택과 취업, 업무를 성별의 관점에서 물었습니다. 참여자들은 처음에는 성별이 진로에 영향을 준다고 여기지 않았으나, 졸업 뒤 전공을 고를 때 직접적인 차별이나 분리를 겪었습니다. 근골격계 과보다 소아과와 피부과에 선호되었습니다. 임신과 출산으로 경력이 끊기고 직장과 가정 모두에서 고정된 성역할을 맡는 일도 확인되었습니다. 저자들은 작은 지역의 여성 한의사가 빠졌다는 점을 한계로 밝혔습니다.

The number of female Korean medicine doctors is rising, yet how gender affects their careers had never been studied. As of 2017 women made up 21% of Korean medicine doctors, against 25.4% of allopathic doctors and a world average of 46%. This study conducted semi-structured interviews with ten female Korean medicine doctors in their thirties, asking about early career choices, employment, job performance, career moves and future aspirations from the perspective of gender, and analysed the transcripts using the constant comparative method of Strauss and Corbin. Participants initially did not regard gender as affecting their career decisions, but after graduation and during employment they met direct discrimination or segregation in choosing fields of treatment and specialty, being preferred for paediatrics and dermatology over departments treating musculoskeletal problems. Pregnancy and childbirth brought career breaks, and stereotypical gender roles followed them into both workplace and home. The authors note as limitations that the experiences of female doctors in relatively small towns are not represented, and that only those who could be contacted and chose to participate were included.
