Factors associated with willingness to receive acupuncture in patients with major traumatic injuries: A qualitative study

Lee D, Cheong MJ, Kim SH, Cho HM, Leem J, Son CH, Shin YK, Kim KH

European Journal of Integrative Medicine 63:102295

DOI

INT73European Journal of Integrative Medicine

논문 소개Summary

중증 외상 환자가 침 치료를 받겠다는 마음을 어떻게 갖게 되는지 근거이론으로 살펴본 질적연구입니다. 국내에서 침이 만성 통증에 흔히 쓰이지만 중증 외상 뒤 환자가 침을 어떻게 받아들이는지는 알려진 것이 거의 없었습니다. 2021년 8월부터 2022년 8월까지 다발성 늑골골절이나 척수손상 뒤 침 치료를 받은 환자 10명을 골라 반구조화 면담을 했고 축어록을 개방·축·선택 코딩으로 분석했습니다. 16개 코드와 6개 하위주제, 개인과 환경의 두 주제가 나왔습니다. 개인 쪽에서는 「회복에 대한 절박함」이 가장 큰 동기였고 양방 의사의 태도가 영향을 주었으며, 환경 쪽에서는 의료기관 접근성과 자원 가용성이 관련되었습니다.

A qualitative study, grounded in grounded theory, of how patients come to be willing to receive acupuncture after major traumatic injury. Acupuncture is regularly used in South Korea for chronic pain, yet little was known about how such patients regard it. Between August 2021 and August 2022, ten patients who had received acupuncture after multiple rib fractures or spinal cord injuries were purposively sampled for semi-structured interviews, and verbatim transcripts were analysed through open, axial and selective coding. Sixteen codes, six subthemes and two themes - personal and environmental - were generated. On the personal side, "desperation to recover" was the primary motivation and the attitudes of Western medicine physicians shaped the decision; on the environmental side, the accessibility of medical centres and the availability of medical resources were associated with willingness.
