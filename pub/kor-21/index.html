Proposal of East-West Integrative Medicine Manual for Rehabilitation after Shoulder Surgery

Song MY, Jo HG, Sul JU, Leem J

Journal of Korean Medicine Rehabilitation 28(1):109-120

DOI

첫 장 · 오픈액세스First page · open access

논문 소개Summary

어깨 수술 뒤 재활에서 한의와 양의가 함께 판단하고 소통하도록 만든 동서협진 매뉴얼입니다. 한 한방병원의 협진위원회가 만든 매뉴얼 가운데 하나로, 한의 재활의학 전문의 두 사람이 초안을 잡고 양방 재활의학 전문의가 검토해 의견을 주고받은 뒤 협진의 목표를 반영해 고쳤으며, 위원회가 검토와 대면 논의를 거쳐 채택했습니다. 관절경적 회전근개 봉합술과 SLAP 봉합술, 관절유리술 뒤의 재활을 다루며 수술 기법에 따른 재활 일정과 시기별 목표, 환자 안내, 수술 후 감염 관리를 담았습니다. 한 의료기관의 결정 과정과 치료 내용을 공개했다는 데 뜻이 있습니다.

A manual of East-West integrative medicine designed so that Korean and Western medical staff can make decisions and communicate together during rehabilitation after shoulder surgery. One of a set produced by the integrative medicine committee of a Korean medicine hospital, it was drafted by two Korean medicine rehabilitation specialists, reviewed by a Western medicine rehabilitation specialist with ideas exchanged, revised to reflect the goal of consultation, and adopted by the committee after review and face-to-face discussion. It covers rehabilitation after arthroscopic rotator cuff repair, SLAP repair and arthrolysis, setting out the rehabilitation schedule by surgical technique, general goals by phase, patient guidance, and management of postoperative infection. Its significance lies in making one institution's decision process and treatment content public.
