An evidence map on traditional medicine across health outcomes

Ang L, Song E, Jong MC, Alræk T, Wider B, Choi TY, Jun JH, Lee B, Choi Y, Lee HW, Yang C, Yim MH, Yamashita H, Ji Z, Hu H, Zhang J, Liu J, Chen Y, Qin Y, Lu L, Qu F, Hansen OM, Kwon CY, Leem J, Lee H, Kim TH, Kim KH, Park S, Lee YS, Jang S, Won J, Choi J, Lee J, Kim SY, Lee MS

논문 소개Summary

전통의학의 체계적 문헌고찰이 어느 질환에 얼마나 쌓여 있는지를 지도로 그린 연구입니다. 연구 우선순위를 정하려면 빈자리부터 알아야 한다는 것이 출발점입니다. 17개 데이터베이스에서 2018년 1월부터 2022년 12월까지를 검색해 241,509건을 찾았고, 중복을 뺀 181,616건을 걸러 20,856건을 전문 평가한 뒤 2,719편을 남겼습니다. 두 사람이 독립적으로 작업했고 질 평가에는 AMSTAR 2를 썼습니다. 성인 대상이 2,591편, 소아는 128편뿐이었고 소화기계·순환기계·비뇨생식계 질환이 가장 많았으며 중재로는 한약 1,867편과 침 471편이 압도적이었습니다. 다만 방법론의 질은 전반적으로 낮았습니다.

An evidence map charting where systematic reviews of traditional medicine have accumulated across health conditions, on the premise that setting research priorities requires first knowing where the gaps are. Seventeen databases were searched from January 2018 to December 2022, retrieving 241,509 records; after removing duplicates, 181,616 were screened, 20,856 went to full-text assessment, and 2,719 systematic reviews were included. Reviewers worked independently in pairs and appraised quality with AMSTAR 2. Most included reviews concerned adults (2,591), with only 128 in paediatric populations; diseases of the digestive, circulatory and genitourinary systems were the most commonly evaluated, and herbal medicine (1,867 reviews) and acupuncture (471) dominated among the modalities. Methodological quality, however, was considerably low overall.
